About Us

Our Story

Sunrise Community Hospital began as a small clinic with one goal: make good healthcare reachable for everyone in our community. Today we offer outpatient, inpatient and emergency care under one roof.

Our Mission

To give safe, kind and affordable care to every patient, and to use technology - like this website and our online booking - to make care easier to reach.

Compassion

Every patient is treated with dignity and respect.

Excellence

We keep our medical standards high.

Integrity

We are honest and open with our patients.
